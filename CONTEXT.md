# Research-to-Repo vocabulary

## Idea

A durable, uniquely identified opportunity for a useful portfolio project,
recorded with its source, discovery history, and lifecycle status.

## Candidate

An Idea evaluated for inclusion in a particular Daily Brief, with a concrete
pitch, evidence, risks, and recommendation.

## Daily Brief

A dated, ranked proposal that presents three buildable Candidates to the human
for selection or deferral.

## Backlog

The set of open Ideas not currently selected for project work, retained for
future comparison and possible resurfacing.

## Selected Project

An Idea the human has chosen to advance through the project lifecycle, from
scope assessment through its recorded outcome.

## Decision Gate

A lifecycle point at which progress requires an explicit human choice or
approval before it may continue.

## Wayfinder Map

A durable map of the major uncertainties, decision tickets, and routes to a
clear plan for a large or uncertain effort.

## Daily Handoff

A durable record of incomplete work that preserves its substantive status,
evidence, blocker or stopping point, and exact next action.

## Shipped

The terminal project status for a verified, documented runnable release that
has passed the required approval and merge events.

## Control Task

The persistent Codex task in which the human receives daily briefs and makes
the consequential workflow decisions.

## Orchestration Repository

The central `research-to-repo` repository that holds durable workflow state,
idea history, briefs, research, system decisions, and the portfolio index.

## Project Repository

The separate repository for one Selected Project, containing its product work
and project-specific decisions, tickets, and delivery record.
